# v0.2.0 - 23 November 2026

Added a theorem characterizing, for every reduced rational parameter, the eventual prime congruence classes where the first Painleve II obstruction vanishes. The resulting density of first-obstructed primes is explicit; two formulas count the residue classes depending on divisibility of the numerator by 3. The b=1 and b=2 cases are settled separately. A new independent arithmetic checker tests the congruence criterion across parameter families and primes.

Also added the exact normalization change connecting Kaneko--Ochiai's PII convention to the standard one, tightened the introduction, harmonized citation metadata and version numbers, and rebuilt the PDF.

# v0.1.2 - 23 November 2026

Established nonzero-polynomial ordering; added the all-large-primes corollary for integer parameters and an exact count of vanishing parameter residues at each p.

# v0.1.1 and v0.1.0

Earlier local preprint revisions concentrated on the first-obstruction formula, arithmetic rational-parameter classification, verification scripts, and proof editing. They are not separate claimed mathematical results.
